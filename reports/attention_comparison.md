## 2. Weekly strategy: Buzz 4 vs equal weight

Every Monday hold the 4 stocks with the most abnormal attention last week (Buzz) or the least (Quiet). Edge = average yearly gap vs holding all stocks equally.

_CAGR, Sharpe and worst drop come from your `metrics.py`; they show – until you write those functions._

## Caveats

- 16 companies chosen because they are famous today (survivorship bias): good past returns are baked in.
- No trading costs. Spike trades and weekly rebalancing both cost money in reality.
- Testing several sources and thresholds and keeping the best is itself a way to find luck. Decide the test before looking, or confirm on data you have not used (Week 4).
