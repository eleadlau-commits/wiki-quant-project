The one test decided in advance

Every Monday, score each stock by how unusual its Wikipedia attention was over the past 7 days. Hold the top 10% (Buzz) for a week. Compare with holding every stock equally, which is the fair benchmark. Charge 0.10% per trade.

How to read this

  • t-stat: the average divided by its uncertainty. Below about 2, luck could easily produce the same number.
  • Out of sample: years not used to design or choose anything. A real pattern should still be there.
  • Luck test: the strategy re-run hundreds of times with the attention scores shuffled between stocks, so they mean nothing. If the real result sits in the middle of those, it is not evidence of anything.
  • By-month t-stat: hundreds of pages spike on the same market-wide news (March 2020). Those bets move together, so they are counted as one month, not hundreds of independent wins.
  • Costs: 0.10% each time you buy or sell, a reasonable estimate for large US stocks. A weekly strategy that swaps most of its stocks pays this many times a year.
